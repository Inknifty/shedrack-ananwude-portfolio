06 — Background

From Customer Service to Product Manager

My path into product management didn’t start in a strategy deck—it started on the front lines of customer service and KYC compliance operations. After supporting 200+ SMEs at Nomba and joining Moniepoint as a KYC Officer in May 2024—where I personally reviewed retail and business accounts, untangled onboarding bottlenecks, and audited complex merchant documentation—I developed an intuitive grasp of where operations break down and where customers feel friction.

In April 2025, I transitioned into product management at Moniepoint, earning rapid internal promotions from KYC Officer to Product Management Intern and full-time Product Manager within 19 months based on operational impact. Today, I own internal fraud prevention platforms, Transaction Monitoring rules, Customer Screening engines, Customer Risk Assessment models, account restriction workflows (Lien, PND, PNC), multi-tenant RFI SDK integrations, and LASS (Loom AI Assistant)—helping analysts cut prep time by ~75% and protecting an estimated ₦500M+ in monthly fraud exposure without introducing friction for legitimate users.

Alongside fintech infrastructure, my volunteering internship at Zarttech designing and shipping consumer and venture platforms like Heilen and 15Wins Venture—combined with volunteering to mentor 150+ aspiring designers at Side Hustle—grounded my approach in deep user empathy, rapid prototyping, and disciplined agile execution.
